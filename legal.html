Legal & Privacy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Privacy

No cookies, no analytics, no accounts. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, comment system, newsletter, form or login. No personal information is collected or shared.

What stays in your browser. On the hosted site, songs you create or save are kept in your browser's local storage (keys starting with strudle:) so they survive a reload. They never leave your device; clearing site data removes them. Exported MP3 and Strudel files are rendered and encoded in the browser and downloaded directly.

Hosting. The site is static and served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL) under the GitHub privacy statement. The author has no access to those logs.

Third-party requests the page makes. Sample packs stream from the Strudel CDN (the same source strudel.cc uses) and, for general MIDI soundfonts, from GitHub. Those services see the requests needed to fetch the audio files, nothing more. The "REPL" button opens your expanded code on strudel.cc, which puts the code in that page's URL; that is a request to a site with its own privacy policy, and it happens only when you press it.

Links. Linked sites (GitHub, LinkedIn, YouTube, the author's blog, Strudel) have their own privacy policies.
