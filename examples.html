How the vocabulary becomes Strudel

Strudle songs are written with a small musical vocabulary: five layers, twelve axes from 0 to 1 where 0.5 is each layer's baseline, descriptors that move several axes at once, modifiers that scale them, and harmony words that pick keys and progressions. Every entry below is playable. Press a variant, listen, then open the expanded Strudel to see exactly what the words turned into. Only one example plays at a time.
