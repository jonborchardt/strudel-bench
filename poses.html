Each frame is the tableau world's own draw of one still shot (web/visual/tableau.mjs: layoutOf places the figures, previewShot builds the shot). Arms between two figures are drawn by the world in canvas space (a sleeve from one figure's shoulder to the other, the hand at its end); arms and hands on one figure are portrait props (web/visual/portrait.mjs PROPS). A theme (web/visual/thriller.mjs) swaps in its own cast, poses and hands, as a song gets with visual: { world: 'tableau', theme: 'thriller' }; with none, this is the editorial sheet as it always was.
