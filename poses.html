poses · framing · set · theme

Each frame is the tableau world's own draw of one still shot (web/visual/tableau.mjs: layoutOf places the figures, previewShot builds the shot). Arms between two figures are drawn by the world in canvas space (a sleeve from one figure's shoulder to the other, the hand at its end); arms and hands on one figure are portrait props (limner PROPS). A theme (web/visual/themes.mjs: a limner cast, its part packs, a limner stance pack and a dance, bound from lib/visual.json) swaps in its cast, its blocking and the stances and hands they name, as a song gets with visual: { world: 'tableau', theme: 'thriller' }; with none, this is the editorial sheet as it always was. This sheet is the blocking, which is this repo's; one body's stance is limner's, on limner/stances.html. The casts are on casts.html, the packs on parts.html.
