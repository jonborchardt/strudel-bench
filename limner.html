limner — what it draws, on its own

limner draws one person: parameters in, an op list out, SVG or canvas from the ops. Nothing on this page knows about music, songs or worlds — it imports ./limner/index.mjs and nothing else from this repo, which is the point. For the parameter-by-parameter editor see portrait.html; for how several figures get arranged into a shot, which is not limner's job, see poses.html.
