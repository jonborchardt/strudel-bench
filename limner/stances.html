stances

Every stance in limner/stances.mjs, one body a cell. A stance is how one body stands — the head's tilt and lean, the turn off the torso, a dropped shoulder, the hands it puts up — and nothing about where that body is in a frame: dx, k and an arm around a neighbour are blocking, which belongs to whatever is composing the shot. Two entries still carry a blocking field and say so. The parts are on parts.html, the casts on casts.html.
